At NightFind, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account or use NightFind, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Date of birth (to verify you are 18+)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Payment information for premium features

1.2 Location Data

NightFind requires access to your device's location to provide core functionality, including:

  • Showing nearby venues, clubs, and bars
  • Creating optimized route plans
  • Providing location-based event recommendations
  • Distance calculations and navigation features

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may limit app functionality. We collect both precise location (GPS) and approximate location (network-based) data when you use the app.

1.3 Usage Data

We automatically collect information about your interactions with the app:

  • Venues you view, save, or visit
  • Search queries and filters used
  • Routes created and navigation history
  • Events you're interested in or attending
  • App features you use and frequency of use

  • App crash reports and performance data

2.1 Supabase

We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure for database management, authentication, and real-time features. Your account data and app content are stored on Supabase servers. Learn more at: supabase.com/privacy

We use Google Maps Platform for location services, mapping, and place data. Google may collect information about your use of mapping features. Learn more at: policies.google.com/privacy

2.3 Apple Services

If you sign in with Apple ID, Apple provides limited information as per your privacy choices. We respect Apple's privacy features including email relay. Learn more at: apple.com/privacy

This Cookies Policy explains how NightFind uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our mobile application and website. By using NightFind, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website or use an application. Cookies help websites and apps remember information about your visit, making your experience more efficient and personalized.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the app to function properly and cannot be disabled. They include:

  • Authentication: Keep you logged in and verify your identity
  • Security: Protect against unauthorized access and cyber threats
  • Session Management: Maintain your session state across app screens
  • Preferences: Remember your language, region, and app settings

2.2 Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how users interact with NightFind, helping us improve the app. These cookies collect anonymous information such as:

  • Pages/screens visited and time spent
  • Features used and user flow patterns
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Crash reports and error logs

3. Third-Party Services

NightFind integrates with third-party services that may set their own cookies:

3.1 Supabase

Our backend infrastructure provider uses cookies for authentication, session management, and database operations. Learn more: supabase.com/privacy

3.2 Google Services

We use Google Maps and Google Analytics, which may collect data through cookies. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/cookies

4. How to Manage Cookies

4.1 iOS Devices

To control tracking on iOS devices:

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security
  2. Tap Tracking
  3. Toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

4.2 Android Devices

To control tracking on Android devices:

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy
  2. Tap Ads
  3. Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
