Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2025

At NightFind, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account or use NightFind, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Date of birth (to verify you are 18+)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Payment information for premium features

1.2 Location Data

NightFind requires access to your device's location to provide core functionality, including:

  • Showing nearby venues, clubs, and bars
  • Creating optimized route plans
  • Providing location-based event recommendations
  • Distance calculations and navigation features

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may limit app functionality. We collect both precise location (GPS) and approximate location (network-based) data when you use the app.

1.3 Usage Data

We automatically collect information about your interactions with the app:

  • Venues you view, save, or visit
  • Search queries and filters used
  • Routes created and navigation history
  • Events you're interested in or attending
  • App features you use and frequency of use
  • Device information (model, OS version, unique identifiers)
  • App crash reports and performance data

1.4 Analytics and Tracking

We use analytics services to understand how users interact with NightFind and improve the app experience. This includes anonymous usage statistics, feature engagement, and performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: Deliver core app functionality, venue discovery, route planning, and event information
  • Personalization: Customize recommendations based on your preferences and location
  • Communication: Send important updates, notifications about events, and promotional offers (with your consent)
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and user experience
  • Safety & Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Age Verification: Ensure all users meet the 18+ age requirement

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

NightFind integrates with third-party services to provide enhanced functionality. These services may collect and process your data according to their own privacy policies:

3.1 Supabase

We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure for database management, authentication, and real-time features. Your account data and app content are stored on Supabase servers. Learn more at: supabase.com/privacy

3.2 Google Services

We use Google Maps Platform for location services, mapping, and place data. Google may collect information about your use of mapping features. Learn more at: policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 Apple Services

If you sign in with Apple ID, Apple provides limited information as per your privacy choices. We respect Apple's privacy features including email relay. Learn more at: apple.com/privacy

3.4 Analytics Providers

We may use analytics services like Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar tools to understand app usage patterns. These services may use cookies and similar technologies.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in these circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who help us operate the app (hosting, analytics, payment processing)
  • Venue Partners: Aggregated, anonymized data with premium venue partners for business insights
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly agree to share your information

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive information
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure authentication via Supabase and Apple/Google sign-in

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Location Control: Manage location permissions through device settings

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@nightfind.com

7. Children's Privacy

NightFind is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18. If we discover that a minor has provided personal information, we will promptly delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers, including compliance with GDPR (for EU users) and other applicable regulations.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 90 days, except where retention is required by law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Your continued use of NightFind after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@nightfind.com
Support: info@nightfind.com
Website: Contact Form

12. Region-Specific Information

For EU/EEA Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For California Users (CCPA)

California residents have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt-out of the sale of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information).

Summary

NightFind collects location data, usage information, and account details to provide nightlife discovery services. We use third-party services (Supabase, Google, Apple) and prioritize your privacy rights. You can control your data through app settings and by contacting us directly.
