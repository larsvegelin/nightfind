Help & FAQ

Find answers to frequently asked questions about NightFind

Welcome to the NightFind Help Center. Browse common questions below or contact our support team for personalized assistance.

Getting Started

What is NightFind?
NightFind is your ultimate nightlife discovery app, helping you find the best clubs, bars, lounges, and events in your city and around the world. Whether you're exploring your hometown or traveling abroad, NightFind connects you with amazing nightlife experiences, complete with route planning, event notifications, and personalized recommendations.
How do I download and install NightFind?
NightFind is available on the Apple App Store for iOS devices (iPhone and iPad). Simply search for "NightFind" in the App Store, or click here to download directly. Android support is coming soon!
Do I need an account to use NightFind?
You can browse venues and events without an account, but creating a free account unlocks additional features like saving favorites, creating routes, receiving personalized recommendations, and accessing your history. Sign up takes just a few seconds using your email, Apple ID, or Google account.
Is there an age requirement?
Yes, you must be at least 18 years old to use NightFind. This is because nightlife venues typically have age restrictions, and responsible use of the app requires adult judgment. We verify age during account creation.

Features & Functionality

How do I plan a route for my night out?
Planning a route is easy with NightFind's smart route planner:
  1. Browse or search for venues you want to visit
  2. Tap the "Add to Route" button on each venue
  3. Open the "My Route" tab to see your planned stops
  4. Drag and drop to reorder venues, or let NightFind optimize the route automatically
  5. Tap "Start Navigation" to get turn-by-turn directions
The app calculates the most efficient path between venues, saving you time and maximizing your night out.
How does location access work?
NightFind uses your device's location to show nearby venues, calculate distances, and provide navigation. Location access is optional but highly recommended for the best experience.

To enable location:
  • iOS: Settings > NightFind > Location > "While Using the App" or "Always"
  • Android: Settings > Apps > NightFind > Permissions > Location
We respect your privacy—location data is only used to enhance your experience and is never sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I search for venues in other cities?
Absolutely! NightFind is an international app with venues worldwide. To search in a different city, tap the location icon at the top of the screen and enter any city name. You can explore nightlife before traveling or plan a night out while on vacation.
How do I save my favorite venues?
To save a venue to your favorites, simply tap the heart icon on any venue card or detail page. Your saved venues are accessible in the "Favorites" tab of your profile, making it easy to revisit your go-to spots or build a wish list of places to check out.
What are premium venues?
Premium venues are clubs, bars, and lounges that pay for enhanced visibility in NightFind. They appear with a "Premium" badge and may be featured at the top of search results. Premium listings often include special perks like exclusive deals, VIP access, or detailed event calendars.

Rest assured, premium status doesn't affect user reviews or ratings—our commitment to honest, unbiased information remains unchanged.
How do I find events happening tonight?
Tap the "Events" tab to see a curated list of parties, DJ performances, theme nights, and special events happening in your area. You can filter by date, genre, venue type, and more. Enable notifications to receive alerts about events at your favorite venues or featuring your favorite DJs.

Account & Settings

How do I edit my profile?
To edit your profile:
  1. Tap your profile icon in the bottom navigation
  2. Tap "Edit Profile"
  3. Update your name, photo, email, or preferences
  4. Tap "Save" to confirm changes
How do I change my notification settings?
To customize notifications:
  1. Go to your Profile > Settings > Notifications
  2. Toggle on/off notifications for events, new venues, promotions, and updates
  3. You can also manage app notifications through your device's system settings
How do I delete my account?
We're sorry to see you go! To delete your account:
  1. Go to Profile > Settings > Account
  2. Scroll to "Delete Account"
  3. Follow the confirmation steps
Account deletion is permanent and will remove all your data, including favorites, routes, and history within 90 days. Alternatively, you can contact info@nightfind.com for assistance.

Pricing & Subscriptions

Is the app free?
Yes! NightFind is free to download and use. The core features—discovering venues, viewing events, creating routes, and saving favorites—are all available at no cost. We also offer optional premium features for users who want an enhanced experience.
What are premium features?
NightFind Premium offers additional benefits, including:
  • Ad-Free Experience: Browse without interruptions
  • Advanced Route Planning: Create unlimited complex routes with multiple stops
  • Exclusive Deals: Access special discounts and VIP offers at partner venues
  • Priority Support: Get faster responses from our support team
  • Early Access: Be the first to try new features
Premium subscriptions are available monthly or annually at discounted rates.
How do I cancel my subscription?
NightFind subscriptions are managed through your App Store account:

iOS (Apple):
  1. Open Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions
  2. Tap on NightFind
  3. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
Android (Google Play):
  1. Open Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions
  2. Select NightFind
  3. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You'll retain premium access until then.
Can I get a refund?
Refund requests are handled by the App Store (Apple or Google) according to their policies. Generally, subscriptions can be refunded within a limited time window if you haven't used premium features. Contact Apple or Google support directly, or reach out to us at info@nightfind.com for assistance.

Safety & Responsibility

How can I stay safe while using NightFind?
Your safety is our top priority. Here are some tips:
  • Go with Friends: Always plan nights out with trusted companions
  • Share Your Location: Let someone know where you're going
  • Use Ride-Sharing: Never drink and drive—use taxis, Uber, Lyft, or public transit
  • Trust Your Instincts: Leave any situation that feels uncomfortable
  • Drink Responsibly: Know your limits and stay hydrated
  • Watch Your Drink: Never leave drinks unattended
If you encounter unsafe conditions at a venue, please report it to venue staff and local authorities.
Does NightFind promote excessive drinking?
No. NightFind is a discovery tool for nightlife experiences, which includes much more than just drinking—live music, dancing, socializing, and exploring culture. We encourage responsible enjoyment and always advocate for safe, legal behavior. Users must be 18+ and should follow local laws and venue policies.

Technical Issues

The app isn't loading venues. What should I do?
Try these troubleshooting steps:
  1. Check Internet Connection: Ensure you have a stable Wi-Fi or cellular connection
  2. Enable Location: Verify location permissions are enabled in device settings
  3. Restart the App: Close and reopen NightFind
  4. Update the App: Make sure you're using the latest version from the App Store
  5. Restart Your Device: A simple restart can fix many issues
If problems persist, contact info@nightfind.com with details about your device and issue.
The app keeps crashing. How can I fix it?
App crashes can be frustrating. Try these solutions:
  • Update NightFind to the latest version
  • Restart your device
  • Clear app cache (if available in settings)
  • Uninstall and reinstall the app (your account data is saved in the cloud)
  • Check for iOS/Android system updates
If crashes continue, please report the issue at info@nightfind.com with your device model and OS version.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
We love hearing from our users! To report bugs or suggest features:
  • Use the in-app "Feedback" option (Profile > Settings > Send Feedback)
  • Email us at info@nightfind.com
  • Visit our Contact page
Your feedback helps us improve NightFind for everyone. Thank you!

Venue & Content

How do I add a venue that's not listed?
If you know of a great venue that's not in NightFind, we'd love to add it! Send us the venue details (name, address, type, website, social media) at venues@nightfind.com or use the "Suggest a Venue" option in the app. We review and add new venues regularly.
How do I report incorrect venue information?
We strive for accuracy, but details can change. If you notice outdated or incorrect information (hours, address, phone number, etc.), please:
  • Tap "Report an Issue" on the venue's detail page
  • Or email venues@nightfind.com with the venue name and correct details
We'll review and update the information promptly.
Are reviews moderated?
Yes, all reviews are subject to moderation to ensure they meet our community guidelines. Reviews containing spam, hate speech, profanity, or false information will be removed. Honest, constructive feedback is always welcome and helps other users make informed decisions.
