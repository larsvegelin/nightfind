Cookies Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how NightFind uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our mobile application and website. By using NightFind, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website or use an application. Cookies help websites and apps remember information about your visit, making your experience more efficient and personalized.

Similar technologies include:

  • Local Storage: Data stored in your browser or app for performance and functionality
  • Session Storage: Temporary data that expires when you close the app
  • Pixels/Beacons: Small images that track user behavior and conversions
  • Mobile Identifiers: Device IDs used for analytics and advertising

2. Why We Use Cookies

NightFind uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Essential Functionality: Enable core app features like authentication and preferences
  • Performance: Understand how users interact with the app to improve performance
  • Analytics: Collect anonymous usage statistics to enhance user experience
  • Personalization: Remember your preferences, location, and settings
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the app to function properly and cannot be disabled. They include:

  • Authentication: Keep you logged in and verify your identity
  • Security: Protect against unauthorized access and cyber threats
  • Session Management: Maintain your session state across app screens
  • Preferences: Remember your language, region, and app settings

These cookies are strictly necessary and do not require consent as they are essential for providing our services.

3.2 Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how users interact with NightFind, helping us improve the app. These cookies collect anonymous information such as:

  • Pages/screens visited and time spent
  • Features used and user flow patterns
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Performance metrics (load times, response times)

Analytics Tools We Use:

  • Firebase Analytics: Google's mobile app analytics platform
  • App Store Analytics: Apple and Google's built-in analytics
  • Third-party analytics providers (anonymized data)

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance functionality and personalization but are not strictly essential:

  • Location Preferences: Remember your preferred search location
  • Favorites: Store your saved venues and routes
  • Filters: Remember your search filters and preferences
  • Theme Settings: Maintain your chosen app theme/appearance

3.4 Advertising Cookies (Optional)

We may use cookies to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad effectiveness. These cookies:

  • Track which ads you've seen to prevent repetition
  • Measure ad performance and engagement
  • Deliver ads based on your interests (with consent)
  • Share anonymized data with advertising partners

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings or within the app.

4. Third-Party Cookies and Services

NightFind integrates with third-party services that may set their own cookies. These include:

4.1 Supabase

Our backend infrastructure provider uses cookies for authentication, session management, and database operations. Learn more: supabase.com/privacy

4.2 Google Services

We use Google Maps and Google Analytics, which may collect data through cookies. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/cookies

4.3 Apple Services

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may use cookies for authentication and security. Learn more: apple.com/legal/privacy

4.4 Social Media Integrations

If we integrate social sharing features, social media platforms may set tracking cookies when you interact with their buttons or widgets.

5. Cookie Duration

Cookies have different lifespans:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies deleted when you close the app (duration: session only)
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted:
    • Authentication cookies: 30-90 days
    • Preference cookies: 12 months
    • Analytics cookies: 24 months

6. How to Manage Cookies

6.1 Mobile App Settings

You can control certain cookie-like technologies through:

  • In-App Settings: Manage analytics and preferences within NightFind settings
  • Device Settings: Control app permissions, tracking, and data collection

6.2 iOS Devices (iPhone/iPad)

To control tracking on iOS devices:

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security
  2. Tap Tracking
  3. Toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  4. Or manage tracking per app, including NightFind

Additionally, manage Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising to control personalized ads.

6.3 Android Devices

To control tracking on Android devices:

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy
  2. Tap Ads
  3. Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  4. Or tap "Reset advertising ID"

6.4 Website Cookies (Desktop)

If you visit the NightFind website, you can manage cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies

6.5 Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies, certain features may not work properly:

  • You may be logged out frequently
  • Preferences and settings won't be saved
  • Some features may be unavailable or limited
  • User experience may be less personalized

Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for core app functionality.

7. Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. Currently, there is no industry standard for responding to DNT signals. NightFind respects device-level tracking preferences (iOS and Android) and compliance with applicable privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA).

8. Children's Privacy

NightFind is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect cookies or tracking data from individuals under 18. If we discover such collection, we will promptly delete the information.
